# Crop Companion & Local Market

A web-based agricultural assistant that helps farmers, gardeners, and agribusiness enthusiasts make better planting decisions using **companion planting insights**, while also connecting them to a **local crop marketplace** for buying and selling produce.

---

## Project Overview

Crop Companion & Local Market is designed to solve two common agricultural problems:

1. **Poor crop yield due to wrong crop combinations**
2. **Limited access to nearby buyers and sellers of farm produce**

The platform provides:
- Smart crop companion recommendations
- An intuitive crop discovery experience
- A localized buy-and-sell marketplace inspired by proximity-based platforms


## Key Features

### Crop Companion System
- View crops and their compatible companion plants
- Filter crops to show **only plants with valid companions**
- Detailed crop modal with:
  - Benefits
  - Companion crops
  - Growth notes

### Local Market (I'm still working on this feature)
- Separate authentication flow for **buyers and sellers**
- Buyers can:
  - Select a crop
  - View nearby sellers offering that crop
